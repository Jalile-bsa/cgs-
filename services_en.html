Our Services

We provide specialized research and engineering services in the field of earthquakes and earthquake-resistant engineering.

Our Main Services

Seismic Research

Conducting scientific research in the field of earthquakes, studying seismic activity in Algeria, and assessing seismic risks at the national level to support decision-makers and urban planners.

  • • Seismic risk studies and preparation of seismic maps
  • • Analysis of seismic data and recordings
  • • Preparation of seismic risk reports for cities and regions

Earthquake Resistant Engineering

Developing standards and criteria for earthquake-resistant construction, and reviewing engineering designs of vital infrastructures such as schools, hospitals, and hazardous warehouses to ensure their safety against earthquakes.

  • • Development of earthquake-resistant construction standards
  • • Review and evaluation of engineering designs
  • • Providing engineering consultancy for construction projects

National Accelerometric Network

Management and operation of the national network of seismic stations for acceleration measurement, and provision of acceleration data and seismic recordings to researchers, engineers, and public authorities.

  • • Operation and maintenance of earthquake measurement stations
  • • Provision of seismic acceleration data
  • • Archiving and recording of seismic events
📋

Risk Reports

Issuance of regular reports on seismic risks for states and regions exposed to seismic activity in Algeria.

🤝

International Cooperation

Cooperation with international and regional organizations in the field of earthquakes and earthquake-resistant engineering, including partnerships with Japanese and European institutions.

🎓

Training and Education

Organizing training sessions and workshops for engineers and researchers in the field of earthquake-resistant engineering and seismic data analysis.
