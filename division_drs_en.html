Regulation and Seismic Risk Reduction Division (DRS)

Development of Algerian seismic regulations.

About the Division

The Regulation and Seismic Risk Reduction Division (DRS) is responsible for developing Algerian seismic regulations (RPA) and supporting national seismic risk prevention policy.

Main Missions

  • Development and updating of Algerian seismic regulations (RPA 99, RPA 2024).
  • Preparation of technical guides (DTR) for seismic engineering.
  • Support for national seismic risk prevention policy.
  • Review of engineering designs of vital infrastructures (schools, hospitals, dams, ...).
  • Provision of engineering consultancy to public authorities in the field of seismic engineering.

Published Technical Products

  • Algerian Seismic Regulations RPA 99 / Version 2003.
  • Design and calculation rules for reinforced concrete structures CBA 93.
  • Execution rules for reinforced concrete works.
  • Design and calculation rules for shallow and deep foundations.
  • Technical guides (DTR) in seismic engineering and geotechnics.
