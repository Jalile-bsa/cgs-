Scientific Divisions

Organization of the center's scientific structure into specialized divisions and subdivisions.

📊

Seismic Hazard Division (DAS)

Responsible for studying seismic hazards, seismic zoning, and risk assessment at the national level.

  • Study of seismic activity in Algeria and risk assessment.
  • Preparation of seismic zoning maps for cities and regions.
  • Seismic risk assessment for infrastructures.
Division details
🏗️

Seismic Engineering Division (DGS)

Responsible for studying building vulnerability, structural system dynamics, and experimental testing.

  • Study of vulnerability and static/dynamic behavior of structural systems.
  • Dynamics of structural systems (buildings, civil engineering works).
  • Experimental testing on structural components and materials.
Division details
🗺️

Seismic Microzoning Division (DMS)

Study of site effects, seismic amplification, and preparation of microzoning maps.

  • Study of site effects and seismic amplification of ground motions.
  • Preparation of seismic microzoning maps for Algerian cities.
  • Stability analysis and landslides.
Division details
📋

Regulation and Seismic Risk Reduction Division (DRS)

Development of Algerian seismic regulations (RPA) and support for national risk prevention policy.

  • Development of Algerian seismic regulations (RPA 99, RPA 2024).
  • Support for national seismic risk prevention policy.
  • Preparation of technical guides (DTR) and engineering books.
