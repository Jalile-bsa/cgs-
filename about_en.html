About the National Earthquake Center

A national applied research establishment specialized in earthquake resistant engineering and seismic research.

Who we are

The National Center for Applied Research in Earthquake Resistant Engineering (CGS) is an Algerian national establishment under the Ministry of Housing, Urban Planning and the City, which aims to develop applied research in the field of earthquake resistant engineering, manage the national accelerometric network, and provide seismic data and risk reports to support urban planning and construction in Algeria.

Our objectives

  • Seismic research: Conducting scientific research in the field of earthquakes and assessing seismic risks.
  • Earthquake resistant engineering: Developing earthquake-resistant construction standards and supporting vital infrastructure.
  • Accelerometric network: Management and operation of the national network of seismic stations and provision of acceleration data.
  • Reports and consultancy: Publishing seismic risk reports and providing engineering consultancy to public authorities.
