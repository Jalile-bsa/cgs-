National Accelerometric Network

National network to monitor seismic activity and record strong ground motions in Algeria.

About the Network

The idea of establishing a national network of accelerographs dates back to 1976, when Algeria decided to set up a national seismic regulation framework. The catastrophic El-Asnam earthquake of magnitude 7.3 (October 1980) served as a catalyst for acquiring accelerographs.

After this "catastrophe" earthquake, it became urgent and necessary to:

  • Monitor seismic activity in Algeria.
  • Record strong ground motions.
  • Understand the physical phenomenon (wave propagation, fault model).
  • Develop attenuation laws.
  • Develop the seismic zoning map of Algeria.
  • Define and model seismic motion for structural calculations.
