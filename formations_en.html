Scientific Seminar Programs

📚

Seminar No. 01: Geotechnical Testing

Static and dynamic properties of soils, different types of soil mechanics tests, interpretation of results, analysis of soil study reports.

🏗️

Seminar No. 02: Shallow Foundations

Bearing capacity (general and special cases), absolute and differential settlements, foundation pathologies.

🏔️

Seminar No. 03: Deep Foundations

Different types of deep foundations (piles, sheet piles, balasted columns, ...), bearing capacity, settlement, quality control.

🌊

Seminar No. 04: Soil Liquefaction: Calculation and Reinforcement

Phenomenon / Causes of liquefaction, assessment of liquefaction potential from geotechnical tests, improvement / reinforcement of soils.

🏜️

Seminar No. 05: Landslides

Definitions and causes of landslides, specific investigations and monitoring methods, stability analysis.

The center plans to organize intensive training cycles soon on the DTR "Algerian Seismic Regulations RPA 2024" and the DTR "Design and Calculation Rules for Foundations and Retaining Structures".

The public will be informed of dates and programs through the website and the Facebook page "Le CGS".
