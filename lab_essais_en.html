Dynamic Structural Testing Laboratory

Testing of structures and structural components to simulate seismic behavior.

About the Laboratory

The Dynamic Structural Testing Laboratory performs testing on structures and structural components to simulate seismic behavior and evaluate structural performance under dynamic loads.

Work Areas

  • Dynamic testing on structural components (beams, columns, connections, ...).
  • Evaluation of structural performance of buildings under simulated seismic loads.
  • Study of behavior of connections and different structural systems.
  • Support for research and development in seismic engineering.
  • Verification of structural calculation models by actual tests.

Tests Performed on Stadiums and Structures

Tests - 5 July Stadium

Dynamic tests on the bleachers of 5 July Stadium to evaluate resistance to seismic and vibratory loads.

Tests - Tizi-Ouzou Stadium

Studies and tests on the folding bleachers of Tizi-Ouzou Stadium to evaluate seismic stability.

Coupler Tests

Static tests on coupler-capital assembly for major works including Djamaa El-Djazair.

Stone and Material Tests

Tests on construction materials and stones used in seismic-resistant structures.

Equipment

  • Dynamic and hydraulic testing devices.
  • Structural response and displacement measurement systems.
  • Software for dynamic experimental data analysis.
