Scientific Laboratories

Five specialized laboratories in seismic research and earthquake resistant engineering.

Geophysics Laboratory

Study of geological structures and geophysics related to seismic activity, including geological and geophysical investigations of active faults.

Lab details

Engineering Seismology Laboratory

Measurement of seismic responses and their impact on structures and buildings, and analysis of structural seismic behavior.

Lab details

Soil Mechanics and Dynamics Laboratory

Study of soil properties and dynamic behavior under seismic loads, including geotechnical testing.

Lab details

Dynamic Structural Testing Laboratory

Testing of structures and structural components to simulate seismic behavior and evaluate structural performance.
