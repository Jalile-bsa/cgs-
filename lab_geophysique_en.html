Geophysics Laboratory

Study of geological structures and geophysics related to seismic activity.

About the Laboratory

The Geophysics Laboratory is one of the five scientific laboratories of the National Center for Applied Research in Earthquake Resistant Engineering. It studies geological structures and geophysics related to seismic activity.

Work Areas

  • Geological and geophysical investigations of active faults.
  • Study of subsurface structures and their impact on seismic wave propagation.
  • Measurement of physical properties of soils and rocks.
  • Preparation of geological and geophysical maps of seismic zones.
  • Support to center divisions in site effect and seismic microzoning studies.

Equipment

  • Soil electrical resistivity and permittivity measurement devices.
  • Light seismic measurement devices (microzoning).
  • Geological and geophysical modeling software.
