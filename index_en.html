About the National Earthquake Center

The National Center for Applied Research in Earthquake Resistant Engineering (CGS) aims to develop applied research in the field of earthquake resistant engineering, manage the national accelerometric network, and provide seismic data and risk reports to support urban planning and construction in Algeria.

The center is located at 252, Kaddour Rahim Street, Hussein Dey, Algiers, and operates under the supervision of the Ministry of Housing, Urban Planning and the City.
