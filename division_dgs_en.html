Seismic Engineering Division (DGS)

Study of building vulnerability and dynamics of structural systems.

About the Division

The Seismic Engineering Division (DGS) is responsible for studying building vulnerability, dynamics of structural systems (buildings, civil engineering works, ...), and experimental testing on structural components.

Five Research Teams

  • Vulnerability and static/dynamic behavior of building structures.
  • Vulnerability of civil engineering works.
  • Vulnerability of installations and major hydraulic works.
  • Metallic structures.
  • Materials and construction processes.

Tasks

  • Static and dynamic analysis of building structures in linear and non-linear domains.
  • Static and dynamic analysis of building structures in three dimensions in linear and non-linear domains.
  • Experimental studies of static and dynamic behavior of buildings or structural elements.
  • Enrichment of Algerian seismic regulations with obtained results.
