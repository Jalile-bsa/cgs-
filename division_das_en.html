Seismic Hazard Division (DAS)

Study of seismic hazards and risk assessment at the national level.

About the Division

The Seismic Hazard Division (DAS) is one of the scientific divisions of the National Center for Applied Research in Earthquake Resistant Engineering, responsible for studying seismic activity in Algeria and assessing its risks at the national level.

Main Missions

  • Study of seismic activity in Algeria and identification of seismic risk sources.
  • Preparation of seismic zoning maps for cities and regions exposed to seismic activity.
  • Seismic risk assessment for infrastructures and structures.
  • Development of seismic risk models and their use in seismic design.
  • Cooperation with other divisions in the development of seismic construction regulations (RPA).

Research Axes

  • Identification and characterization of active faults in Algeria.
  • Assessment of seismic amplification and site effect on ground motions.
  • Preparation of probabilistic and deterministic risk maps.
  • Analysis of data from the national accelerometric network.
